The Master of Public Policy and Global Affairs students are passionate about leveraging the skills and knowledge developed from their experiences and the program to pave the future of policymaking. This includes striving to impact both local and global policies, inspiring social development, or mobilizing change for a more equitable and just world. Our students are early to mid-career professionals coming from around the world, fostering a diverse community, each with their unique experience to offer the program.
