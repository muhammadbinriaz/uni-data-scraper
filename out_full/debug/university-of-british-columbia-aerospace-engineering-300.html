Aerospace engineering

This class comprises research and experimental development in aerospace engineering.

Parent Category

Mechanical engineering

Sub-Categories

Aerodynamics (except hypersonic aerodynamics)
Aerospace materials
Aerospace structures
Aircraft performance and flight control systems
Avionics
Flight dynamics
Hypersonic propulsion and hypersonic aerodynamics
Satellites, space vehicles and missiles design and testing
Aerospace systems design
Aerospace engineering, n.e.c.

Bushe, William Kendal

Department of Mechanical Engineering, Faculty of Applied Science

Faculty (G+PS eligible/member)

Numerical modelling and mechanical characterisation; Automotive combustion and fuel engineering (including alternative and renewable fuels); Hypersonic propulsion and hypersonic aerodynamics; Aerospace engineering, n.e.c.; Numerical Simulation of Turbulent Combustion; Reacting Flows; Energy Conversion Systems; clean energy

Kravchenko, Sergii

Department of Materials Engineering, Faculty of Applied Science

Faculty (G+PS eligible/member)

Composite and hybrid materials; Polymers and plastics engineering; Aerospace materials; Aerospace structures; Polymers and polymer composites; Multi-functional composites; High-throughput manufacturing process modelling of composites; Multi-scale, multi-physics, probabilistic computational modelling of composites; Manufacturing-informed performance simulation of composites; Machine learning methods in composites manufacturing and performance analysis; Composite structures for renewable energy & unmanned vehicles; additive manufacturing
